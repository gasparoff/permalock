This is the first post of the example blog. It talks about why addresses on the web matter more than people think.
A URL is a promise: whoever links to it expects it to keep working tomorrow, next year and in ten years. Search engines
remember it, social networks keep it in old posts, and language models quote it in their answers. Breaking it costs
visitors, ranking and trust, and nobody notices until the traffic graph goes down.
