How the scan works

  1. Find what you publishedEvery URL the Wayback Machine saw answer 200, back to the first capture. A URL only in a sitemap is not counted.
  2. Ask your site todayUp to 200 of those URLs, requested now, following every redirect.
  3. Sort the answersA page or one redirect is fine. Chains are slow, and a 404 is a broken link out there.

Never lose another URL

Permalock keeps a lockfile of every URL your site publishes. Moved pages get a redirect, deleted ones stop the build until you decide.

npx permalock init

Built in the open

Permalock and this scanner are open source. A star helps other people find it. If this scan looks wrong, tell us: odd sites make the scanner better.
